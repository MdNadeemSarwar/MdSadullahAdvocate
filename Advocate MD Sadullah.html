Common questions

Before you call

How is bail applied for?

The application goes to the court that is hearing the case, and in some cases to a higher court. The court looks at the charge, the facts and whether the accused will attend the trial. Bring the FIR, any arrest papers and an ID, and the advocate will tell you which application fits your case.

How long does a divorce by mutual consent take?

Both spouses file a joint petition, and a second motion follows after a waiting period. The law sets this period at six months, and a court can sometimes shorten it. The total time depends on the court and on how complete the papers are.

Which papers should I bring to the first meeting?

Bring every notice, order, FIR, agreement and receipt related to the matter, your ID, and the papers of any earlier case. A simple folder in date order saves time.

I live in Bihar or Noida. Can the advocate still take my case?

Yes. Matters from Delhi, Delhi NCR and Bihar are all taken up. You can speak to the advocate by phone or WhatsApp before you decide.
